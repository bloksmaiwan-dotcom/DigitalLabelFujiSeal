Where does this bottle go?

    Why you can leave the band on

    The band is made of a light plastic film. In the recycling plant it floats off in the water bath, while the bottle sinks and is made into a new bottle.

    Nutrition

    Velde contains no energy, fat, sugar or protein. What it does contain are natural minerals from the source.

    Where it comes from

    Velde comes from Bron Veldhoven, a protected spring in North Brabant. Rainwater filters slowly through layers of sand and clay before it reaches the source, which gives Velde its soft, mild taste.

    The water is bottled at the source, without any treatment except filtering out sand.
