How to recycle this bottle

    Why you can leave the band on

    The band is made of a light plastic film. In the recycling plant it floats off in the water bath, while the bottle sinks and becomes a new bottle.

    Ingredients

    Carbonated water, colour (caramel E150d), acids (phosphoric acid, sodium citrate), sweeteners (aspartame, acesulfame K), natural flavourings including caffeine.

    Contains a source of phenylalanine. High caffeine content is not recommended for children and pregnant or breastfeeding women.

    Where it comes from

    Made and bottled in the Netherlands for the Dutch market.

    The bottle

    The bottle is made from 100% recycled PET, apart from the cap and band. The brand's shape is pressed into the plastic, so the bottle needs much less label to be recognised.
